Make the dashboard of `stars dev` clickable. A click on a channel or a level of the sidebar shows or hides it like `Space`, a second click or an `Alt`/`Ctrl` click solos it like `s`, a click on a group header folds the group, a click on a key of the list runs that key, and the wheel scrolls the logs. The new `dev.mouse` option (default `true`) and `stars dev --no-mouse` leave the mouse to the terminal.
